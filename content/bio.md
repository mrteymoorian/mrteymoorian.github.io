🧑🏻‍💻 I am a Ph.D. candidate in  [Computer Sciences](https://www.cics.umass.edu) at [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://www.umass.edu), working with Prof. [Amir Houmansadr](https://people.cs.umass.edu/~amir/) in the Secure, Private Internet (SPIN) Research Group.

🔬 My research focuses on the security and safety of embodied and agentic AI systems. I study vulnerabilities in reasoning-enabled systems—including autonomous agents, vision-language-action models, and multi-agent systems—as well as privacy and provenance in generative AI.

My work has been published at USENIX Security and includes collaborations with researchers at Qualcomm and Cisco.
